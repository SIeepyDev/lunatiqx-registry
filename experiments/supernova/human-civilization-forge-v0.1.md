# HUMAN × CIVILIZATION FORGE v0.1
2026-10-19 | Draft simulation specification | Parent: SUPERNOVA control plane

## Worlds
1. Human Gym: strength, speed, recovery, skill, and sustainable training. Simulate overload, fatigue, rest; don't infer personal medical fitness.
2. Intellectual Gym: recall, spacing, deliberate practice, calibration, problem-solving, transfer tests. Avoid false generalized IQ claims.
3. Pattern Forge: learn pattern families; mix domains; reserve unseen tests; penalize spurious correlations.
4. Founder Studio: hypothesis, prototype, customers, cash runway, iteration, governance. Resolve boldness vs evidence, not blind defiance.
5. Civilization Collider: population, disease, energy, institutions, trade, information, conflict, environment, shocks; multiple interpretations and sensitivity runs.
6. Economic Systems Arena: markets, commons, cooperatives, command planning, mixed systems; simulate incentives and externalities without claiming inevitability.
7. Historical Epoch Explorer: influence is multidimensional (military, economic, cultural, technological, diplomatic). Don't assign universal deterministic rankings.
8. Culture and Anime Forge: time-period story settings, original creative assets, licensing provenance, production stages, game adaptation.

## Transfer engines
STRENGTH -> adaptive overload -> education -> escalating difficulty with recovery.
PERIODIZATION -> scheduling -> worker orchestration with rest and inspection.
RETRIEVAL PRACTICE -> runbooks -> test an agent's actual ability rather than trusting documentation.
SPACED REPETITION -> knowledge maintenance -> periodic revalidation of stale tool schemas.
SCIENTIFIC CONTROLS -> civilization counterfactuals -> vary one mechanism and run seeded comparisons.
INSTITUTIONAL ACCOUNTABILITY -> connector audit -> trace all actions with authority and receipts.
NATURAL SELECTION (descriptive analogy) -> prototype tournament -> evaluate variation subject to constraints; never treat selection as moral justification.

## Epistemic rules
Generational labels and cultural norms are descriptions, not destiny. Society is path-dependent, contingent, influenced by ecology, technology, institutions, historical coercion, trade, culture and chance.
Collapse is rarely a single factor or total disappearance. Trace continuity of languages, people, tools, and institutions.
Physical gym performance differs from intelligence, subject expertise, creativity, and wisdom; train and test separately.
Period effects, age effects, and birth-cohort effects are difficult to identify independently.
Economic systems are mixtures with varying property rights, state capacity, legal institutions and informal practices.

## Initial game design
Browser-side Causality Gym: three tracks (Body, Mind, Civilization); sliders; reversible seeded scenarios; compare outcomes. Must distinguish pedagogical heuristic from real-world prediction.
