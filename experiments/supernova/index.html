OUR STANDARD

Build something worth remembering.

Human vision + AI assistance

Two perspectives, independent evaluation, accountable creation. Luna is an AI identity, not a separate legal authority.

Evidence over mythology

Speculative lore is welcome. Real work earns its status through running builds, clear sources, reproducible tests and honest results.

Originality over imitation

Study great games and creative work; publish original assets, licensed material and lawful functional interpretations.
